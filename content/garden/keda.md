[Keda](https://keda.sh/). Application autoscaling made simple We **assess** it under **[[Tool]]** in the garden.  It monitors external systems and internal CRDs for triggering events.  Once triggered it recacluates scale requirements and updates the Deployments, or HPAs accordingly.

## Blurb

> Kubernetes Event-driven Autoscaling

## Summary

**When to use:** When your application that doesn't scale based on items measurable by an HPA.

**When to skip:** When a simpler alternative already covers the need like an HPA.
