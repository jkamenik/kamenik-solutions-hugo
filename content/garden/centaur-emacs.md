[Centaur Emacs](https://github.com/seagle0128/.emacs.d) is a batteries-included [[Emacs]] configuration aimed at a clean, fast out-of-the-box IDE feel. We **assess** it when evaluating Emacs distributions rather than building `init.el` from scratch.
