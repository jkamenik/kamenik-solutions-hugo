[Skaffold](https://skaffold.dev/). Reports itself as "Fast.

## Blurb

> Easy and Repeatable Container & Kubernetes Development

## Summary

**Garden stance:** We **trial** Skaffold for our estate.

**Key points:** As a developer tool for a certain type of org it is clearly categorized as "adopt". However, despite Google's insistence to the contrary, it is not a good [[Continuous Deployment]] tool. It will always need to be replaced eventually, but we find that the same mindset choose skaffold for CD has often taken other shortcuts that make migration harder than one would expect. Thus we are cautious.
