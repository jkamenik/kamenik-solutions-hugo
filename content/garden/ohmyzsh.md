[Oh My Zsh](https://ohmyz.sh) is a community framework for managing the Zsh configuration. We **adopt** it as the shell setup on top of [[Zsh]].

## Blurb

> Oh My Zsh is a delightful, open source, community-driven framework for managing your Zsh configuration.

## Summary

Use Oh My Zsh to manage aliases, plugins, and themes on top of Zsh. It has been the setup since Apple switched the default shell, and its config ships through the stowed dotfiles.

## Details

- **Upstream:** https://ohmyz.sh
- **Role:** framework for the Zsh configuration in the TUI IDE stack
- **Bundled:** 300+ plugins and 150+ themes
- **Related:** [[Zsh]], [[Dotfiles in Version Control]]
