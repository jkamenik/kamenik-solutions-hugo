[Spacemacs](https://www.spacemacs.org/) is a community [[Emacs]] distribution centered on Evil (Vim) keys, mnemonic leader bindings, and curated layers. We **assess** it when Vim muscle memory should drive an Emacs stack.

## Blurb

> Emacs advanced Kit focused on Evil

## Summary

Use Spacemacs when you want discoverable `SPC`-leader mnemonics and a crowd-configured layer system on top of Emacs. Skip it if you want stock Emacs keybindings ([[Emacs Prelude]]) or a non-Emacs AI IDE ([[Cursor]]).

Trade-off: strong ergonomics and consistency vs a heavy, opinionated configuration. Pairs conceptually with [[Vim]] habits while still living inside [[Emacs]].

## Details

- **Upstream:** https://www.spacemacs.org/
- **Source:** https://github.com/syl20bnr/spacemacs
- **Core idea:** Evil editing + mnemonic prefixes (`b` buffer, `p` project, `s` search) + layers
- **Fit:** [[Tool]] / [[IDE]] as an Emacs kit, not a separate editor binary
- **Contrast:** [[Centaur Emacs]] (GUI polish, not Evil-first), [[Emacs Prelude]] (closer to vanilla Emacs)
