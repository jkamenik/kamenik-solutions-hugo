[RemNote](https://www.remnote.io/) is a note-taking and spaced repetition system that combines hierarchical note-taking with active recall flashcards for learning and memory retention. We assess it for its integration of knowledge management and cognitive science-based memorization.

## Blurb

> RemNote is a spaced-repetition wiki that helps you remember everything you write using AI-powered scheduling and hierarchical note-taking.

## Summary

**When to use:** Choose RemNote when you need integrated note-taking with spaced repetition for learning, studying, or memorizing facts embedded in your knowledge base.

**When to skip:** Opt for Obsidian or Logseq if your primary need is general knowledge management without spaced repetition integration.

**Key trade-offs:**
- **Pros:** Integrated SRS (spaced repetition), hierarchical notes, AI scheduling, free tier available
- **Cons:** Less mature than dedicated tools, learning curve for SRS concepts, smaller community than Obsidian
