[SilverBullet](https://silverbullet.md/) is a programmable, browser-based knowledge database for personal and team use. We assess it for data sovereignty and extensibility.

## Blurb

> SilverBullet is a Programmable, Private, Browser-based, Open Source, Self Hosted, Personal Knowledge Database.

## Summary

**When to use:** Use SilverBullet for privacy-sensitive knowledge management and programmable note-taking where you control the data.

**When to skip:** Choose simpler tools when you prefer hosted solutions with automatic backups and updates.

**Key trade-offs:**
- **Pros:** Maximum privacy, programmable with Lua, self-hosted, offline capability
- **Cons:** Requires self-hosting setup, learning curve for Lua scripting, manual backups

## Details

| Topic | Notes |
|-------|--------|
| **Architecture** | Single self-contained server; runs on Raspberry Pi or VPS |
| **Scripting** | Space Lua dialect for commands, templates, widgets, and queries |
| **Features** | Live preview, wiki links, tasks, objects, and integrated query language (SLIQ) |
| **Deployment** | Binary or Docker; SilverBullet+ offers a desktop experience |
