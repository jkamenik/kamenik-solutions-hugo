[Architecture Decision Record (ADR)](https://www.cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions) An [Architecture Decision Record](https://www.cognitect.com/blog/2011/11/15/documenting-architecture-decisions) captures one significant architectural decision, its context, and its consequences. We **adopt** ADRs because AI-assisted delivery increases the need to preserve why a system took its current shape.
