[Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/) is a platform we **assess** in the garden.  This provides a set of challenges including required data.  It allows you to design models, agents and processes to compete and be ranked.

## Blurb

> The World's AI Proving Ground

## Summary

**When to use:** If you are a model or tool developer and what to see how your tool compares to others.  Or if you are evaluating models and AI tooling and want to evaluate various options.

**When to skip:** If you are anyone else.
