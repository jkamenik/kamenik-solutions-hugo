Under **[[Tool]]**, **Chaos Tools** groups products that inject controlled failure so teams find resilience gaps before production incidents. Typical targets are **[[Kubernetes]]** workloads and cloud services. Tag a product here when the note is about the fault-injection runner or platform, not about the broader **[[SRE]]** practice.
