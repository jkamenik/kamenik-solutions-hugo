[Roam Research](https://roamresearch.com/) is a note-taking tool that uses a block-based, bi-directional linking system designed for networked thought and knowledge management. We trial it for its unique approach to graph thinking and backlinking.

## Blurb

> Roam Research is a note-taking tool for networked thought. A graph database that works in the browser and stores data as plain text, allowing you to link ideas across pages and discover unexpected connections.

## Summary

**When to use:** Choose Roam Research when you want bi-directional linking, graph visualization, and a block-based approach to knowledge work that emphasizes connections over hierarchy.

**When to skip:** Opt for Obsidian if you prefer local-first control and a richer plugin ecosystem, or simpler tools if you don't need networked thought.

**Key trade-offs:**
- **Pros:** Bi-directional links, graph view, block references, daily notes, web-based with local export
- **Cons:** Cloud-first (data stored on Roam servers), subscription pricing, limited offline support, not local-first
