[Anki](https://apps.ankiweb.net/) is an open-source spaced repetition flashcard program for long-term memorization of facts and concepts. We assess it as a dedicated SRS companion to our knowledge tools.

## Blurb

> Anki is a program which makes remembering things easy.

## Summary

**When to use:** Choose Anki for serious, high-volume spaced repetition study where retention matters more than integrated note-taking.

**When to skip:** Skip it when you want notes and SRS combined in one place; RemNote integrates both.

**Key trade-offs:**

| Pros | Cons |
|------|------|
| Mature SM-2 scheduling | Separate tool from your notes |
| Free, open source, sync included | Dated UI |
| Huge shared deck ecosystem | Card authoring is manual |
| Extensible via add-ons | |

**Related:** [[PKM]] [[RemNote]]

## Details

| Topic | Notes |
|-------|--------|
