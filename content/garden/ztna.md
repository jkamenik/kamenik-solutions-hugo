Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) grants application-level access based on identity and policy instead of traditional perimeter VPNs. We **assess** it under **[[Technique]]** in the garden.

## Summary

**When to use:** Evaluate on a project when the capability clearly fits the requirement.

**When to skip:** When a simpler alternative already covers the need.
