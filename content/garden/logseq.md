[Logseq](https://logseq.com/) is a privacy-first, open-source outliner and knowledge base that works on local plain-text Markdown and Org-mode files. We assess it for agent-compatible workflows and its outliner-first approach.

## Blurb

> A local-first, non-linear, outliner notebook for organizing and sharing your personal knowledge base. Use it to organize your todo list, write your journals, or record your unique life.

## Summary

**When to use:** Choose Logseq when you prefer an outliner-first workflow with daily journals, block-level linking, and open file format (Markdown/Org).

**When to skip:** Opt for Obsidian if you want a richer plugin ecosystem, or simpler tools if outliner structure feels foreign.

**Key trade-offs:**
- **Pros:** Outliner-first, block references, daily journals, open source, local files, whiteboard feature
- **Cons:** Smaller plugin ecosystem than Obsidian, steeper learning curve for non-outliners, mobile app still maturing
